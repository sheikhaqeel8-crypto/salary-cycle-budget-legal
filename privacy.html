Privacy Policy

Salary Cycle Budget · Last updated: 18 November 2026

This policy explains how the Salary Cycle Budget app ("the app") handles information. Developer contact: lvtraders004@gmail.com.

Information you enter

The app stores the information you enter, such as income, expenses, categories, billing cycle settings and history, only on your device. We do not run servers that receive this data. We do not sell, rent or share it.

No accounts, ads or analytics

The app does not require an account. It does not show ads and does not include advertising or analytics tools. If you choose to turn on Google Drive backup, you sign in with your own Google account (see below).

PIN lock

If you turn on the PIN lock, a scrambled (hashed) form of your PIN is stored on your device only. We cannot see or recover your PIN.

Reports and backup files

PDF reports and backup files are created on your device. They leave your device only when you choose to share or save them, for example to Google Drive, WhatsApp or another app. Those services have their own privacy policies. If you delete the app without saving a backup file, your data on the device is deleted.

Google Drive backup (optional)

If you turn on Google Drive backup, the app asks you to sign in with your Google account and to allow access to the app's private data area in your Google Drive (the "drive.appdata" permission). The app can only see its own backup file there, named salary-cycle-budget-backup.json, and cannot see your other Drive files. The backup file contains the data you entered in the app. It is stored in your own Google Drive and is sent directly from your device to Google. We do not receive a copy and we cannot access it. The app uses your Google account email address only to show which account is connected. You can turn this off at any time in Settings.

Subscription and payments

Subscriptions are sold and managed by Google Play. We do not receive or store your payment card details. Google Play tells the app whether your subscription is active. You can cancel at any time in Google Play: Payments & subscriptions > Subscriptions.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

Your choices and how to delete your data

You control your data. You can edit or delete entries inside the app, or remove all app data by using Reset in Settings, clearing the app's storage, or uninstalling the app.

To delete a Google Drive backup: turn off Google Drive backup in the app's Settings, then in the Google Drive app go to Settings > Manage apps (or visit your Google Account > Security > Third-party access) and delete the hidden app data for Salary Cycle Budget. You can also remove the app's access to your Google account there. We do not keep any copies of your data on our servers.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page.
