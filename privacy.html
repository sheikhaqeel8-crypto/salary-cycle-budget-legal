Information you enter

The app stores the information you enter, such as income, expenses, categories, billing cycle settings and history, only on your device. We do not run servers that receive this data. We do not sell, rent or share it.

No accounts, ads or analytics

The app does not require an account or sign-in. It does not show ads and does not include advertising or analytics tools.

PIN lock

If you turn on the PIN lock, a scrambled (hashed) form of your PIN is stored on your device only. We cannot see or recover your PIN.

Reports and backup files

PDF reports and backup files are created on your device. They leave your device only when you choose to share or save them, for example to Google Drive, WhatsApp or another app. Those services have their own privacy policies.

Subscription and payments

Subscriptions are sold and managed by Google Play. We do not receive or store your payment card details. You can cancel at any time in Google Play: Payments and subscriptions, then Subscriptions.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

Your choices

You can edit or delete entries inside the app, or remove all app data by clearing the app storage or uninstalling it.
